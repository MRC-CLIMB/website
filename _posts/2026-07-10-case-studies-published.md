We've published a set of case studies showing how CLIMB infrastructure
has been used in practice — from national-scale pandemic surveillance to
yearly bioinformatics training cohorts:

- **[CLIMB-COVID]({{ '/docs/case-studies/climb-covid/' | relative_url }})** — supporting the COG-UK consortium's analysis of over two millions SARS-CoV-2 genomes.
- **[mSCAPE]({{ '/docs/case-studies/mscape/' | relative_url }})** — UKHSA's metagenomics surveillance and analysis programme.
- **[PATH-SAFE]({{ '/docs/case-studies/path-safe/' | relative_url }})** — farm-to-fork surveillance of foodborne pathogens and AMR.
- **[training]({{ '/docs/case-studies/training/' | relative_url }})** training the next generation of bioinformaticians.

Read the full set on the [case studies page]({{ '/docs/case-studies/' | relative_url }}).
