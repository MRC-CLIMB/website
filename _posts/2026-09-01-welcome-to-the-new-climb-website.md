We've rebuilt climb.ac.uk from the ground up: a minimal, fast site
focused on what researchers actually need — what CLIMB offers,
what it costs, and how to get started.

Technical documentation hasn't moved — everything for working on CLIMB
day to day (JupyterLab, storage, Nextflow, and more) still lives at
[docs.climb.ac.uk]({{ site.docs_site }}), kept up to date by the team.
This site is the front door: pricing, packages, onboarding, our
[case studies]({{ '/docs/case-studies/' | relative_url }}), and — new —
this news page, for announcements, funding updates and stories from the
CLIMB community.

If there's something you'd like to see here, [get in touch]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).
