## Our mission

CLIMB exists to democratise access to cutting-edge computational resources
for all bioinformaticians, no matter their career level. We aim to
accelerate scientific discovery by providing researchers with the
infrastructure, tools and support needed to analyse their data efficiently
and effectively.

## Our history

CLIMB was launched in 2014 with initial funding from the Medical Research
Council (MRC) as MRC-CLIMB (Cloud Infrastructure for Microbial
Bioinformatics), established to address the growing computational needs of
microbial genomics researchers. Following its success, CLIMB-BIG-DATA was
awarded additional funding to expand its capabilities and serve the broader
microbial bioinformatics community.

For over a decade, CLIMB has supported research in microbial
genomics, including pivotal contributions to pathogen surveillance during
disease outbreaks and ongoing public health initiatives — most visibly as
the infrastructure behind the UK's COVID-19 genomic surveillance effort.

In 2025, CLIMB entered a new phase of growth and sustainability,
consolidating its position as a reference point for bioinformatic research
and surveillance in the UK and expanding its services to reach a global
audience.
