<ul class="steps">
  <li>
    <h4>2014 — MRC-CLIMB starts</h4>
    <p>Founded with initial funding from the Medical Research Council (MR/L015080/1)to address the growing computational needs of microbial genomics researchers.</p>
  </li>
    <li>
    <h4>2016 - Official CLIMB launch</h4>
    <p>Over 100 groups in the UK signed up fo use CLIMB within the first 5 months.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>COVID-19 — CLIMB-COVID</h4>
    <p>Built within the existing MRC-CLIMB infrastructure to support the COVID-19 Genomics UK (COG-UK) Consortium — NHS organisations, UK public health agencies, over 20 academic partners, and regional sequencing centres including the Quadram Institute (QIB). Enabled analysis of over two millions SARS-CoV-2 genomes for outbreak management and vaccine research. See the <a href="{{ '/docs/case-studies/climb-covid/' | relative_url }}">CLIMB-COVID case study</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>CLIMB-BIG-DATA</h4>
    <p>Following its early success, CLIMB was awarded additional funding (MR/T030062/1)to expand capabilities and serve the broader microbial bioinformatics community beyond the original consortium.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>2025 — a new phase, towards self-sustainability</h4>
    <p>CLIMB entered a new phase of growth and sustainability, consolidating its position as a reference point for bioinformatic research and surveillance in the UK, and expanding its services to reach a global audience.</p>
  </li>
</ul>

<p>See how CLIMB has supported specific programmes and consortia in our <a href="{{ '/docs/case-studies/' | relative_url }}">case studies</a>.</p>
