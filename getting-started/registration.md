Primary users — independent researchers and team leaders at UK academic
institutions, government agencies or healthcare systems — can register a team
via Bryn. Secondary users (students, postdocs, collaborators) then join by
invitation from their team's primary user.

<div class="callout amber">
  <p><strong>Only primary users should register to create a new team.</strong> Secondary users are invited, not registered directly.</p>
</div>
