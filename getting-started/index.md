<ul class="steps">
  <li>
    <h4>Register</h4>
    <p>Primary users register a team via Bryn. Secondary users are invited by their team's primary user. See <a href="{{ '/getting-started/registration/' | relative_url }}">Registration</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>Set up authentication</h4>
    <p>Two-factor authentication is mandatory and set up on first login. See <a href="{{ '/getting-started/authentication/' | relative_url }}">Authentication</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h4>Launch resources in Bryn</h4>
    <p>Launch notebook servers, create S3 buckets and manage your team from the Bryn dashboard. See <a href="{{ '/getting-started/bryn/' | relative_url }}">Using Bryn</a>.</p>
  </li>
</ul>

## Who can use CLIMB?

<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Category</th><th>Who</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><strong>Primary users</strong></td><td>Independent researchers and team leaders in UK academic institutions, government agencies or healthcare systems, with an <code>ac.uk</code>, <code>gov.uk</code> or <code>nhs.uk</code> email address. Can register and lead a team.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Secondary users</strong></td><td>Students, postdoctoral researchers and international collaborators working under a primary user. Join by invitation only.</td></tr>
    <tr><td><strong>Industrial users</strong></td><td>Commercial researchers — <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">contact us</a> for terms and pricing.</td></tr>
