Bryn is the CLIMB user portal — where you register, log in, and from where
you launch notebook servers, manage S3 buckets, and administer your team.

<div class="grid-2">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Notebook servers</h3>
    <p>Launch a JupyterLab environment, choose CPU/RAM, and open it once it's running.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Compute usage</h3>
    <p>See your team's CPU usage against your quota.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>S3 storage</h3>
    <p>Create and manage S3 buckets, and share access with collaborators.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Team management</h3>
    <p>Invite secondary users, manage permissions, and renew your licence.</p>
  </div>
</div>
