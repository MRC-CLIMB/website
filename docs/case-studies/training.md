Several Institutes runs yearly bioinformatics training sessions on
CLIMB infrastructure. As with all our other training partnerships, attendees
get an easy-to-use JupyterLab environment with access to Jupyter
notebooks, RStudio and Nextflow to analyse data — no local setup required.
