The Pathogen Surveillance in Agriculture, Food and Environment (PATH-SAFE)
Programme developed a national surveillance network to detect and
track foodborne human pathogens and associated antimicrobial resistance
(AMR), from farm to fork.

More than 65 organisations from government, academia and industry were
involved, including the Quadram Institute (QIB) and the University of
Birmingham (UoB). CLIMB has provides the cloud infrastructure used to analyse
data across the programme.

Read more on the [Food Standards Agency's PATH-SAFE programme page](https://www.food.gov.uk/our-work/pathogen-surveillance-in-agriculture-food-and-environment-path-safe-programme).
