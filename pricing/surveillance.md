CLIMB has run at national-surveillance scale before — CLIMB-COVID
supported analysis of over two millions SARS-CoV-2 genomes for the
COG-UK consortium, and CLIMB infrastructure continues to underpin
programmes like PATH-SAFE and mSCAPE. Capacity can be optimised to support a national capability when it's needed.

## What's included

- Dedicated compute capacity, sized to the surveillance programme
- Multi-institution, multi-team access with role-based permissions
- Reproducible pipelines for continuous data harmonisation and integration to public databases
- Rapid scale-up path for outbreak response

## Pricing

Surveillance programmes are typically multi-institution and
multi-year — pricing is agreed directly with the lead agency or
consortium based on scope, partner count and compute requirements.
