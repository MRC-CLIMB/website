Running a course or workshop? A training package gives every attendee a
ready-to-use JupyterLab environment. No local installs, no SSH keys, no
"it works on my machine."

## What's included

- A sandboxed team created for your cohort, with attendees invited as secondary users
- Ability to pre-load materials on shared team or S3 storage
- JupyterLab, RStudio and Nextflow available out of the box
- Environment torn down at the end of the course

CLIMB already provides resources for recurring training partnerships, including yearly
sessions for the Master's and DTP's programmes —
see the <a href="{{ '/docs/case-studies/' | relative_url }}">case
studies</a>.

## Pricing

Training packages are quoted per cohort, based on group size, duration and
compute needs. Get in touch with your dates and expected attendee numbers and we'll return a quote.

## Training Starter Pack

A dedicated training account including:
- Minimum of 10 participants (access to 4 vCPUs each)
- 500GB Team Shared Storage
- 1TB S3 Storage
- 1 week early access for the tutors to set up the environment

Starts at £35 per participant per week (excl. VAT)
